About the Scheme

Our Aim / प्रमुख उद्दिष्टे

Our aim is to provide quality modern education to rural and urban students — especially those who are underprivileged and financially disadvantaged. BVPY makes free, accessible education a reality for every deserving child.

आमचे ध्येय ग्रामीण व शहरी भागातील विद्यार्थ्यांना गुणवत्तापूर्ण व आधुनिक शिक्षणाची संधी उपलब्ध करून देणे आहे. विशेषतः दुर्लक्षित व आर्थिकदृष्ट्या मागास विद्यार्थ्यांसाठी शिक्षण मोफत व सुलभ करण्याचा आमचा प्रयत्न आहे.

How to Apply — 3-Step Process

1
Step 1
Fill Application Form
  • Available online at www.bvpy.in
  • At Regional Authorised Office
  • Distributed in schools by authorities
2
Step 2
Attend Scholarship Exam
  • 50 Questions — 100 Marks
  • Duration: 1 Hour (60 Minutes)
  • Mode: Online / Offline
  • Subjects: Maths, Science, English, GK
  • All questions are compulsory
3
Step 3
Interview & Verification
  • Qualified candidates called for interview
  • Visit nearest authorised centre
  • Submit required documents
  • Verification takes 3 working days
  • Qualified candidates receive ID number
Required Documents for Verification
Mandatory Documents
  • Student's Aadhar Card (Xerox)
  • Parent / Guardian's Aadhar Card (Xerox)
  • Father / Guardian's PAN Card (Xerox)
  • Caste Certificate or Income Certificate (Xerox)
  • 10th Marksheet (if available)
Important Notes
  • Documents verification will take 3 working days.
  • Only one attempt will be calculated for results.
  • Online exam must be given on internet-accessible device.
  • Qualified candidates will receive a unique ID number.
